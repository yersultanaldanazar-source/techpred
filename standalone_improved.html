A note from the team

Picture arriving in Almaty with no family nearby and a semester starting in two weeks. You call listing after listing, and the answer keeps being "we don't rent to students." Twelve non-local students described exactly this when we interviewed them, and a third of them said they were turned away for being a student.

We're three Narxoz students, and we've made this our problem to test. Students who have a brother or a relative in the city mostly avoid it, so we want to give everyone else that same person. This page is a small pilot, not a finished company. If you try it, tell us honestly how it went, and we'll keep going only if it actually helps.

— Claudio, Yersultan & Yerkezhan, Team SUA
